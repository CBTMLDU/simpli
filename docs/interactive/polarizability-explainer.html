Why we accept this Fixed charges are tuned to behave correctly in ordinary water, which is where most of a protein sits. So the error is small most of the time — and the model is far cheaper.
Where it costs us In unusual surroundings: buried protein interiors, metal binding sites, membranes, and the strongly negative environment around DNA.
