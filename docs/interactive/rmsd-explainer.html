The arithmetic is simple. For each atom, measure how far it sits from where it was in the reference. Square those distances, take the average, then take the square root: RMSD = √( Σ|rᵢ − rᵢref|² ⁄ N ). Squaring is what makes RMSD sensitive to a few badly displaced atoms — one atom moving 4 Å counts far more than four atoms moving 1 Å each.
A two-dimensional model with four atoms, for clarity. Real RMSD works the same way in three dimensions, over thousands of atoms.
