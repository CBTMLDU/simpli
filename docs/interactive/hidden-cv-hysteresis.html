You have chosen one coordinate, and it looks reasonable. A restraint drags the system from state A to state B and back, and the work it takes is the profile. Watch the two curves. If they land on top of each other, you have your answer. If they do not, something you are not measuring is lagging behind.
What is actually there. Two coordinates. x is the one you chose — a distance, say. y is a gate that has to swing open before the crossing is cheap, and it moves on its own timescale, set by the slider. Every profile on this page is built only from x, exactly as it would be in a real study. Nothing in that output reports the existence of y.
