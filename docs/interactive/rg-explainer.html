Try "Folded", then "Unfolded". Watch the green circle grow as the chain opens out. That circle is Rg — when the atoms sit further from the centre, the number goes up.
The arithmetic. Find the centre of the molecule, measure how far every atom sits from it, square those distances, average them, take the square root: Rg = √( Σ mᵢ|rᵢ − centre|² ⁄ Σ mᵢ ). Heavier atoms count for more, which is why it is called mass-weighted. The two component numbers add up neatly: Rg² = (spread x)² + (spread y)² — in three dimensions there is a z as well.
A two-dimensional chain of twelve atoms, drawn with equal masses for clarity. Real Rg works the same way in three dimensions, with real atomic masses.
